# Security Policy

## Security-First Commitment

TrustNode is an enterprise-grade, self-hosted AI-powered Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) platform. Security is the core foundation of our product and our development practices. We appreciate the work of security researchers and practitioners in keeping the community safe and secure.

## Supported Versions

Only the latest release of the TrustNode Community Edition is actively supported with security patches.

| Version | Supported | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| v1.x (Current) | :white_check_mark: Yes | Active development and support |
| < v1.0.0 | :x: No | Unsupported pre-release builds |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not open public GitHub issues for security-related vulnerabilities.** Instead, report all security issues through our secure channels.

To report a vulnerability:
1. Send an encrypted or plain-text email to our security response team at [security@trustnode.io](mailto:security@trustnode.io).
2. Provide a detailed description of the vulnerability, including:
   - Steps to reproduce or proof-of-concept (PoC) code/scenarios.
   - The impact of the vulnerability.
   - Any proposed remediation details.

We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours and work with you to coordinate a security release timeline.

## Disclosure Policy

TrustNode follows a coordinated vulnerability disclosure (CVD) process. We ask that researchers:
- Give us reasonable time to investigate and mitigate the issue before public disclosure.
- Do not exploit the vulnerability beyond what is necessary to prove its existence.
- Do not access, modify, or delete any customer, user, or platform data.

Our team will issue CVE identifier(s) where applicable, and credit you for your discovery in our security advisories and release notes.
